Commissioner runbook

Keep this page beside your contest controls. Use your browser’s Print command to save a paper copy or PDF.

On the shared display, choose Participant view ↗. On that page, open Commissioner and sign in. Reveal and results controls appear above that section after lock.

1. Before lock

  1. Check the contest name, code, slate and displayed lock time. For the revised September 12, 2026 contest, lock is noon Eastern / 11 a.m. Central.
  2. Open the shared display on the TV. Have a player scan its QR code and confirm it opens the intended contest.
  3. Under Commissioner → Join requests, choose Approve on site, Approve remote or Decline.
  4. Check completion status. Remind players to finish all 15 selections and the final-score prediction, wait for autosave, then choose Submit card. Submitted cards remain editable until lock.

Confidence picks are straight-up winners, with 1–6 used once each. Picks stay private even from the commissioner. At lock, valid completed picks still count on an incomplete card; unanswered picks score zero. Joining and editing close at the deadline.

2. Restore a player’s access

  1. Confirm who is requesting access. Use Restore player access under Commissioner and select their existing player name.
  2. Choose Generate recovery code and share it privately with that player.
  3. The player opens the same contest, chooses Already joined? Recover your picks and enters the code.

Codes work once and expire after 15 minutes. A new code replaces the previous one. Redemption restores the same card and signs out older sessions. Recovery works after lock, but does not reopen editing. If a code expires or its response is lost, generate another. Avoid creating a duplicate player.

3. Run the Reveal

  1. At the deadline, keep the contest open. Active pages pick up the locked Reveal state on refresh.
  2. Use Next Reveal step to move through lock, consensus, lone wolves, Upset Special, confidence stakes and Main Event. Pause for reactions; aim for about 2–4 minutes overall.
  3. Choose Finish Reveal and publish all picks when ready. This releases full picks to everyone and opens Live.

Reveal is manually paced. Phones and the TV follow the same step. Refreshing does not restart it; full cards remain hidden until the final step.

4. Watch scores and correct a result

The revised September 12 contest is configured for automatic CFBD polling about once a minute, from noon Eastern Saturday through 4 a.m. Sunday. Browser views refresh about every three seconds. A different contest needs its own provider setup.

Latest provider change means the last changed observation, not the last successful poll. An unchanged timestamp or clock alone does not prove the feed is broken. PTS are banked; PROJ adds points implied by live scores, not a forecast. On the shared display, click another live game tile to feature it.

  1. If an observed result is wrong or missing, open Commissioner results entry and select the correct game.
  2. Check the team labels. Set In progress or Final, and enter observed scores. For the Main Event, also retain or fill in all known first-score and halftime facts.
  3. Enter a clear Source / reason for entry or correction, then choose Save commissioner override. Verify the displayed result and recalculated standings.
  4. When the provider is reliable again, select the game, enter a reason, and choose Remove override and use CFBD. Verify the restored result.

An override replaces the whole game’s facts, including Main Event fields, and remains authoritative until removed. Recheck every field before saving; blank fields are not merged from the provider. Removing an override with no provider observation returns that game to unresolved.

Use game status Void — cannot resolve today for a cancellation or postponement that cannot be settled that day. Its affected picks score zero. Do not void a game merely because a feed update is late.

5. Check the Main Event

Confirm the first team to score, first scoring play, halftime leader and final score. A final score alone cannot establish the first score or halftime result.

When entering the first score, provide both the team and play type. Use OTHER for a known scoring play other than a touchdown or field goal; use VOID only when that proposition cannot be resolved that day. Halftime can be a team, TIE or VOID.

An exact total-line tie scores zero for everyone; an ATS push scores one point. Winning-path examples describe possibilities, not probabilities.

6. Finalize once results are checked

  1. Confirm every game is Final or Void and all Main Event facts are resolved or explicitly void.
  2. Review scores, overrides, standings and the final-score tiebreak. Correct any mistakes now.
  3. In Commissioner results entry, choose Finalize standings and confirm. If disabled, look for unfinished games or unresolved propositions.
  4. Verify the champion or co-champions and final standings.

Finalization is permanent in the current app. It saves the final result, stops automated updates and closes commissioner result entry. There is no reopen control. Do not finalize while a result is still disputed.

Quick troubleshooting

  • “Enter the contest code”: reopen the full contest link or scan the correct pregame QR code. A bare site URL does not identify a contest.
  • Missing commissioner controls: use Participant view, open Commissioner and sign in. Reveal/results controls appear after lock; results editing closes after finalization.
  • Refresh or connection error: check connectivity, reload, then inspect the latest state before repeating an action. For a stale/conflicting update, review refreshed values and retry if still needed.
  • Scores disagree with the broadcast: verify the game and observation time, check for a Commissioner override, then use the result correction steps above.
  • TV is stuck on one game: click another live tile. The display keeps its selected game while it is live; automatic impact-based switching is not implemented.
  • Player has lost their card: recover the existing player’s access. Recovery preserves picks and does not bypass lock.
